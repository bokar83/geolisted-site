How the build runs

Phone-log and form-submit intent monitoring, routed to your dispatch board.

  1. Step 1 / Discovery

    We pull your real numbers

    30-minute call to look at your current call log, miss rate, average ticket, and CRM setup. If the math does not work, I tell you on the call and we stop there. No discovery fee.

  2. Step 2 / Strategy

    Scoped build plan + recovery target

    I send a written scope: which calls get rescued, which forms get monitored, where the qualified leads land in your dispatch queue, and what the 30-day target looks like for your specific shop.

  3. Step 3 / Build

    Twilio + Vapi + n8n + your CRM

    AI voice agent answers missed calls within 2 rings, qualifies the lead (name, address, problem, urgency, callback window), and pushes the job into your dispatcher's queue tagged AI-rescued. Built on your accounts. You own the stack.

  4. Step 4 / Launch

    Live in 2 weeks, your CSR trained

    SMS reminders fire 15 minutes before each callback. Your CSR or tech calls back with full context. We monitor the first 14 days closely and tune the voice agent based on real calls.

  5. Step 5 / Ongoing optimization

    Monthly recovery report and tuning

    Each month you get a one-page report: calls rescued, callbacks booked, jobs closed, revenue recovered. I tune the voice agent on real conversations. Cancel the retainer anytime, the system keeps running.
