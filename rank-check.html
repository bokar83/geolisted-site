Common questions

We send 6 real customer queries (e.g., "best chiropractor in South Jordan UT") to 4 AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google Gemini) and read the responses. We count how many times your business name appears across the 24 total answers, and we log everyone else who gets named.

Honest answer: those engines have no public API for ranking-style queries. Anyone claiming to track Siri, Alexa, or Google Assistant visibility is either selling vapor or scraping in a way that breaks against their terms. We only track what is reproducibly measurable. ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini have real APIs, real consumer adoption, and stable outputs we can sample.

0 to 25%: AI engines do not see you. Customers asking AI for your service in your city never hear your name.

25 to 50%: Occasional. You show up on specific-service queries (e.g., "sports chiropractor near me") but not the high-volume "best [business type]" searches.

50 to 75%: Regular. AI engines reliably name you across most query variants.

75%+: Dominant. You are one of the named go-to options in your city.
